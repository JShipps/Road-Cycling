Questions

What kind of bike do I need?

A road bike or a fast gravel bike with road tires, in good working order. Flat bars are fine in the social group.

What happens if I get a puncture?

The group stops and waits. Ride leaders carry spares and will help you fix it.

Will I get dropped?

Not on a club ride. Every group regroups at the top of each climb and at junctions, and the sweep rides at the back.

Do rides go ahead in the rain?

Yes, unless there's ice, snow or a severe weather warning. Ride leaders post cancellations in the club chat by 7:00.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Email the membership secretary and we'll refund the months you haven't used.
