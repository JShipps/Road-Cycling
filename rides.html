Group riding basics

  • Hold your lineRide predictably. Brake gently and avoid sudden swerves so the wheel behind can trust you.
  • Point and callSignal potholes, gravel and parked cars. Pass the call back down the line.
  • Two abreast, single when askedThe back rider calls "car back". Single out quickly and smoothly when it's safe.
  • Half-wheel nobodyKeep your bars level with your partner's. Pushing the pace splits the group.
  • Wait at the topEvery climb ends with a regroup. Nobody rides on until the leader counts everyone in.

Bring with you

  • Road bike in working order
  • Helmet (required on every ride)
  • Spare tube, levers and a pump or CO₂
  • Two bottles and food for 2+ hours
  • Phone, card and an emergency contact

Meeting point

Old mill parking lot, Station Road. We gather by the noticeboard 10 minutes before roll-out.
