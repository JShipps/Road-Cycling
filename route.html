Hollow Gap Loop

The Saturday classic. A long drag up to Hollow Gap, rolling roads to the café at Ashby, then one last kick over Ashby Rise before the run home along the river.

Distance
84km
Climbing
1,040m
High point
598m
Ride time (B group)
3:05

Climbs

  • km 15Hollow Gap8.2 km at 5.1%
  • km 54Ashby Rise3.4 km at 6.3%
  • km 72Weir Lane0.6 km at 9.0%

Turn-by-turn

  1. 0.0Leave the old mill parking lot, turn left onto Station Road
  2. 3.4Right at the mini roundabout towards Fenwick
  3. 14.8Left onto Gap Road. The climb starts at the cattle grid
  4. 23.1Regroup in the lay-by at the summit
  5. 31.6Careful: sharp right-hand bend at the bottom of the descent
  6. 44.0Café stop at Ashby village green
  7. 54.2Right onto Ashby Rise, then straight over at the crossroads at the top
  8. 71.8Left onto Weir Lane, short steep ramp
  9. 84.0Finish back at the old mill parking lot

Good to know

  • Café: The Green Kettle, Ashby. Card only.
  • Water refill at the Ashby church tap.
  • Gap Road descent is gritty after rain.
  • Bail-out: skip Ashby Rise via the county road, saves 9 km.

Ride it with us on Saturday

Roll-out 8:00 from the old mill. Three pace groups.
