Six riders and a café table

Tailwind started in 2014 as a Saturday ride between friends. It now has over 200 members and rides five days a week.

How we got here

We wanted a club where a newer rider could join a fast group ride without being dropped on the first hill. The regroup rule came first; everything else followed.

We're affiliated with the national cycling federation, carry club insurance for all members, and train ride leaders in first aid every spring.

  1. 2014

    First Saturday ride: six riders, one route, a lot of cake.

  2. 2016

    Club formally founded. First kit order and the Tuesday chaingang.

  3. 2019

    Social group launched for new riders. Membership passes 100.

  4. 2023

    Route library online with 40 routes checked by ride leaders.

  5. Today

    200+ members, four pace groups, five rides a week.

Club kit

Slate blue with a pink profile line across the chest. Orders open twice a year, in March and September, and members get the jersey at cost.

You don't need kit to ride with us. Any bright, comfortable cycling clothing is fine.
